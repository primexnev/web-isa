About Me

I am a BSc Immersive Technologies student at HSLU in Switzerland. I make games: mobile games and web-based games that run directly in the browser.

I work with Unreal Engine and Unity, build 3D models in Blender and bring them to the web with Three.js. I care about games that are fun, clear and easy to start.
